About me

I'm Veer Pratap, a 16-year-old student in the 10th grade at Ocean Pearl International School. I like figuring things out — taking something apart, seeing how it works, then trying to build my own version of it.

Right now that mostly means the web. This site is one of those projects, and it's an honest picture of where I am: learning by doing, one thing at a time. I'm not an expert yet and I don't pretend to be. I'm just early — and I'm enjoying it.

I'll be straight with you: I'm not the strongest student in class. I learn by making things and breaking them, not by memorising — which is great for projects and less great for exams. I'm working on both.

When I'm not studying I'm usually in Minecraft or Free Fire, or watching anime — Naruto is the favourite. Building something inside a game and then wanting to build the real thing is honestly why this site exists in the first place.

One more thing, since it explains a lot: I'm an introvert. Big groups drain me, and I'll say more in one message than I would out loud all week. Hand me something to build on my own and I'm completely happy.

Rajput
